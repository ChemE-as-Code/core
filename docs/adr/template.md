# NNNN. Short title in the imperative or as a noun phrase

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN (link the superseding record) | Deprecated
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD (the date the decision was taken)

## Context

What forces are at play: the problem, the constraints (technical, licensing, people), the
options that were considered. Neutral, factual, short.

## Decision

What we decided, in active voice ("cheme-as-code does X"). Name the rule precisely enough that a
reviewer can tell whether a change follows it.

## Consequences

What becomes easier and what becomes harder. List both:

- Positive: ...
- Negative: ...
- Known limitations and open follow-ups (link the issues).

## Evidence

- PR or commit that introduced the decision (`#NNN`, short hash).
- Files that implement or enforce it (code, tests, docs).
