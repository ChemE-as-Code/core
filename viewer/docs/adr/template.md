# NNNN. Title in the imperative or as a noun phrase

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated | Superseded by NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD (the day the decision was taken)

## Context

What forces are at play: the problem, the constraints (technical, project, people), and what
the decision has to satisfy. Neutral; no decision yet.

## Decision

What we decided, in active voice ("The viewer ..."). One decision per record.

## Consequences

What becomes easier and what becomes harder, including the negative consequences and the
follow-up work the decision creates.

## Evidence

Commits, pull requests, issues and files that show the decision (short hashes, `#N`).
